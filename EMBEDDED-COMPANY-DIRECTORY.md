Always verify roles, locations, legal entities, and active vacancies on the company careers page.

## How to use this directory responsibly

1. Start with 20–30 companies matching your chosen role, not all 300+.
2. Search role terms precisely: `firmware`, `embedded software`, `BSP`, `device driver`, `validation`, `application engineer`, `RTL`, `DV`, `physical design`, or `DFT`.
3. Verify that the job is in the country/city you can work from and that the legal entity matches the listing.
4. Match your resume and project evidence to the actual job description; do not claim every technology in the company domain.
5. Track application date, role, referral/contact, outcome, and follow-up in a personal sheet.

## Notes on curation

- Brand families, acquisitions, subsidiaries, and country-specific entities may appear separately in source data when they can be relevant to job searching.
- Some entries are broad technology or engineering employers rather than dedicated semiconductor businesses.
- Duplicated rows and repeated Mobis/Menta entries in the supplied source were removed from this directory.
